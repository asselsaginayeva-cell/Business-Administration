Where Bayan Sulu stands

No public proof of AI, big-data tools or a customer-tracking system yet.

That is a gap — but also a clean start. There is no old system to replace, and the company already invests steadily in equipment and certificates (ISO 9001, ISO 22000, ISO 50001, Halal).[2]

why this pair?

I grew up with Bayan Sulu sweets, and I wanted a Kazakh company with real public numbers — it is listed, so it has to publish them. Ferrero was the natural contrast: same products, but it already runs AI in its supply chain and marketing. Every recommendation on this site is for Bayan Sulu; Ferrero only supplies the evidence.
